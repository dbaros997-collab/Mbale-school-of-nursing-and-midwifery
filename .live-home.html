About
Programs

Registered school · Nursing & Midwifery focus

Where you learn to nurse and midwife with skill and heart.

Mbale School of Nursing and Midwifery combines classroom teaching, skills labs, and hospital placements in Mbale.Registered with the Ministry of Education and Sports. Accredited by UNMC and NCHE.

How we teach

You learn by doing. Tutors guide you in class, in the lab, and on the ward so you are ready for the work ahead.

Where you learn

Teaching blocks, skills labs, a computer lab, and spaces built for nursing and midwifery training.

  • Quality

    MBSNM meets UNMC and NCHE standards. Experienced tutors, solid programmes, and real ward practice — that is what we offer.

  • Reliability

    We are a registered school. You can count on clear guidance, fair processes, and staff who take your training seriously.

  • Innovation

    Skills labs, hospital placements, and digital learning tools help you grow into a nurse or midwife who can serve your community well.

About MBSNM

Our story

Christians from GREM started MBSNM when they saw how much Eastern Uganda needed community health care. We train nurses and midwives to serve the poor, the young, and the elderly — In God We Love and Serve.

“In God We Love and Serve”

  • Compassion

    Patient-centred care, always.

  • Competence

    Clinical skill proven in practice.

  • Faith

    Service rooted in Christian conviction.

  • Community

    Health for the poor, young and elderly.

  • Integrity

    Honest, accountable professionals.
