Welcome to Mbale School of Nursing & Midwifery.

Mbale School of Nursing and Midwifery was started by Christians of Global Revival Evagelistic Ministries (GREM) after realizing the need of providing health care services to the community especially the poor, the young and elderly through community health services. Mbale School of Nursing and Midwifery is a child born after a dream made possible through sharing with other Christians of GREM.
